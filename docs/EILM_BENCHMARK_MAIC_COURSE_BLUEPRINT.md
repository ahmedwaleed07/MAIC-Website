## Benchmark and scope

- Source index: https://eilm.edu.eu/all-courses/
- Additional logistics syllabus examples: https://eilm.edu.eu/courses/introduction-to-logistics-and-supply-chain-management/ and https://eilm.edu.eu/courses/procurement-and-sourcing-strategies/
- EILM publicly lists eight six-course Mini-MBA tracks and nine four-course Programme Certificates. The 27 distinct titles within the eight tracks plus five logistics titles provide 32 distinct course references. Duplicates within programmes are one course, not separate copies.
- Public course landing pages publish outlines or lesson names; complete restricted lesson text was NOT accessed. These MAIC module plans are independently written instructional designs, NOT copies/translations of EILM lessons. MAIC has no institutional affiliation with EILM.
- Course catalogue entries are all marked **preparing**, are self-paced proposals, and do not accept enrollment or claim actual content delivery.
- Programme labels and certificate criteria must be separately approved. MAIC offers completion/participation credentials only, not academic degrees, and should not imply accreditation.
- Prices, instructor names, durations, multilingual localization (including Kurdish), assessment details, content licensing, photos and materials remain unapproved, unpopulated or pending.
- Website published catalogue comes from Cloudflare D1 Admin Studio and can override `courses.js`. This PR updates ONLY the repository fallback draft; publishing to visitors requires an authorized Admin Studio content revision.

### Business Administration / إدارة الأعمال

**Finance and Accounting Essentials — أساسيات المالية والمحاسبة** (`finance-and-accounting`)

1. Accounting records — السجلات المحاسبية
