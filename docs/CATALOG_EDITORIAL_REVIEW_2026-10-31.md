# MAIC catalog editorial change — 31 October 2026

## Why this change

The MAIC library should avoid books that clearly promote a competing course provider through its branded marketing pages. Original authors, academic affiliations, publishers, quotations, citations and licence notices are not grounds for exclusion. MAIC must never remove required author/publisher attribution or falsify origin.

## Replacement

The original `human-marketing-09` entry (`eMarketing: The Essential Guide to Marketing in a Digital World — Seventh Edition`) was published by Red & Yellow Creative School of Business and contains its school branding and direct marketing links. It is replaced in the library with `Customer Centric Strategy — Second Edition` by Kerri Shields, a separately verified CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 open textbook. The original academic author and licence are retained in metadata, and the book links directly to the source PDF. This does **not** rebrand the book as MAIC-owned content.

## EILM topic inspiration

Source for subject categories, not reused course files or lesson text: https://eilm.edu.eu/all-courses/ and https://eilm.edu.eu/courses/ .

32 independently authored MAIC course outlines cover business, leadership, IT management, project and productivity, innovation, strategy, international governance, finance, professional communication and logistics/supply chains. Four existing MAIC introductory courses are preserved. All 32 added titles have English and Arabic descriptions, three learning outcomes, four syllabus units and entry requirements. Course material, instructor, duration, fees, assessments and translated lessons **are not yet ready**; all added entries remain `preparing` and cannot be represented as accredited degree courses or copied Mini-MBA qualifications.

Changing the GitHub fallback does not update an already published D1 document. The Cloudflare D1 publication must be updated separately with revision control, preservation of other CMS sections and a history entry.
